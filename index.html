Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a price quotation?

- Send approximate window dimensions (width & height) or window photos to our WhatsApp.

Do you provide on-site measurement and installation?

- Yes, we provide on-site measurement and installation services. Our team will visit your location to take accurate measurements and ensure a perfect fit for your blinds.

What is the difference between manual and motorized blinds?

- Manual blinds use a durable roller chain/gearbox, while motorized systems can be controlled via remote or smart app.

Which areas do you cover?

- We cover the entire country of Malaysia, including Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, Penang, Johor, and other states.

How long does fabrication take?

- Usually 7–14 working days from finalized measurement to installation.
